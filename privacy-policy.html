Privacy Policy — Blink Bill

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

Blink Bill ("we", "our", "the app") is a billing and business management application built for shop owners and small businesses in India. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Blink Bill is designed to work primarily on your device. The information handled by the app includes:

  • Shop & Business Details: Shop name, address, and settings you enter to personalize your billing.
  • Billing Data: Products, prices, quantities, discounts, GST/tax details, and generated bills.
  • Customer Information: Customer names, contact numbers, and purchase history that you choose to save for your own record-keeping.
  • Stock & Inventory Data: Product details, barcodes, and QR codes you add for inventory management.
  • Expense Records: Business expenses you log within the app.
  • UPI Payment Details: UPI IDs you configure for generating payment QR codes. We do not process, store, or have access to your actual payment transactions — payments happen directly through your UPI app.
  • Camera Access: Used only to scan product barcodes. We do not store camera footage or images beyond what's needed to read the barcode.

2. How We Store Your Data

Blink Bill primarily stores your business data locally on your own device. This means your billing records, stock data, and customer history stay on your phone/browser unless you choose to back it up.

  • Backup & Restore: If you use the Backup Data feature, your data is saved as a file that you control. You are responsible for keeping backup files secure.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your business or customer data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Blink Bill integrates with the following third-party services to provide core functionality:

  • WhatsApp: When you use the "Share via WhatsApp" feature, bill details are shared through WhatsApp's own platform, subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • UPI Apps: Payment QR codes generated in Blink Bill are processed by your chosen UPI payment app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.), subject to their respective privacy policies.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the data handled within the app. However, since a significant portion of your data is stored locally on your device, we recommend:

  • Keeping your device secured with a lock screen or password.
  • Regularly backing up your data using the app's Backup feature.
  • Not sharing your device or login access with unauthorized persons.

5. Your Rights & Control

  • You can view, edit, or delete your business, product, and customer data at any time within the app.
  • You can back up or restore your data at your discretion.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time, which may remove locally stored data depending on your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Blink Bill is intended for business use by shop owners and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
